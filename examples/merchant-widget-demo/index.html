Acme Store — My Account

This page pretends to be a merchant's own logged-in "My Account" page. It never handles a phone number, an OTP code, or a Packeta credential — everything below the button is Packeta's own widget, mounted natively into this page (no iframe). Once identified, the widget itself also lets the customer view transactions/installments and deposit or pay an installment (both go through ZarinPal, so those two actions briefly leave this page).

Checkout

A second, unrelated widget: the "Pay with Packeta" checkout, same charge your server already knows how to open — either embedded flat into the page, or as a bottom sheet that slides up over it. Same PacketaPay.init() call either way; just a presentation option.
