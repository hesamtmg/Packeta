Acme Store — My Account

This page pretends to be a merchant's own logged-in "My Account" page. It never handles a phone number, an OTP code, or a Packeta credential — everything below the button is Packeta's own hosted widget in an iframe. Once identified, the widget itself also lets the customer view transactions/installments and deposit or pay an installment (both go through ZarinPal, so those two actions briefly leave this page).
